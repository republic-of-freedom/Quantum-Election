Put math inside $…$ in the problem. Each step is one line: caption :: LaTeX. Supported: \frac, ^, _, \sqrt[n]{ }, \int, \sum, \prod, \lim, \left( \right), \boxed, \text{ }, Greek letters, \pm, \cdot, \le, \ge, \to, \infty, \quad and more.

Each share has one value per period. Bar and Pie show the period you pick; Trend, Line and Scatter plot every period. CSV layout: a header row of share names, then one row per period.
